# Cite-before-answer checklist

Before typing your final answer, run this check internally:

1. Call EvidenceList. Confirm it is non-empty.
2. For each claim in your planned answer, identify the evidence id(s) that
   support it.
3. If any claim has no id → either remove the claim, or go gather one more piece
   of evidence.
4. Prefix your final answer with `Citations: e1, e2, …` listing the ids you
   used.

A final answer with zero citations is invalid on research tasks. Do not guess.
